# BETTERWATER -- `ext.better_water`: a shore edge on rivers and lakes

Block BETTERWATER, 2026-10-31. Class A, default ON (as every class A row, DESIGN.md §2), live; settings key
`ext.better_water`, display "Better water", description "A soft shore edge on rivers and lakes: shallow water
at the bank and a thin line of foam at the waterline." Off under `/profile:faithful`. The Enhanced tab's
combo is Off / Plain; Fancy (below) joins it as a third item with its own valued key when it is built.

Ruled with the user: a subtle look (no stylized foam bands); Plain for every backend and for Android
(power); Fancy for desktop.

## 1. The retail facts it rests on

- **The water already fades in from the bank.** The River and Lake materials (`RiverMaterial.srsl:Normal_ps`,
  the Lake material uses the same pixel program with `Normal_lakevs`) sample the frame copy taken before the
  water (`frame`, t1) and finish with `lerp(frameBuffer.xyz, lighting, frameBuffer.w)`.
- **The frame copy's alpha is a shore depth.** The terrain (`TerrainMaterial.srsl`, `output.color.w =
  GetUnderWaterAmount(z)`) and the in-water models (`OpaqueMaterial.srsl:SnowWater*`, the OpaqueInWater
  material) write `GetUnderWaterAmount` (`functions.srsl`: 0 at z = -0.75, rising linearly to 1 at z = -2.25)
  into the colour alpha; ordinary opaque models write 0. Read behind a water pixel, it is the vertical
  depth below the waterline of whatever lies behind it: world-space, and it does not move with the camera
  (the depth-difference technique of the usual shoreline shaders, already computed).
- **Two contour ranges, two materials.** `WorldGeometry` draws the water mesh in two range tables: mode
  0x6000 (`_contourRangesA`, `_waterMaterialA`) holds the phase-1 flow bodies (rivers, the coast), mode
  0x8000 (`_contourRangesB`, `_waterMaterialB`) the phase-2 shore rings round lakes and ponds
  (`water_contour_mesh.cpp`). Their vertex programs scroll the two uv sets differently (`Normal_vs`:
  y += 0.02 t, w += 0.01 t; `Normal_lakevs`: y -= 0.0057 t, w += 0.01 t, z += 0.005 t).
- **The water's interpolants** (`RiverMaterial.srsl` `Interpolant`, compiled to `TEXCOORD0..4` in struct
  order): texcoord, viewDir (world - camera), screenProjection, lightFogAlpha (x/y lighting, z fog,
  w the mesh alpha), shadowProjection.

The kit's `.srsl` sources were read as a reference (DESIGN.md §3 rule 7); nothing of them is in the tree.

## 3. How it draws

**The second draw.** Right after retail draws a contour range, `DrawShore` reads the water program the
material bound (`Context::_pixelConstantStaging.videoProgram`), binds its twin (`VideoProgram::SetupShaders`),
draws the same range again, and binds the water program back. Retail's water draw is not touched.

**The twin.** A `Video::VideoProgram` of ours, made with the factory's own `CreateNewObject` (the class
`operator new` is not ported), holding the water program's vertex layout, vertex program and render state
(the user's installed ones: alpha blend, depth LessEqual) and our `Video::PixelProgram`, whose `_byteCode[0]`
is the generated DXBC. Every backend creates and binds it as a program read from a package (DX11 takes the
DXBC; Vulkan converts it with dxbc-spirv, Metal with airconv; both read only `_byteCode`): no backend code.
Twins are made on first use on the drawing thread, kept in an eight-slot table recycled round robin, and
matched on the water program and the four parts copied from it, so a world load or a device restart
(`MainLoop::OnVideoRestart`, new programs, often at the same address) makes a fresh twin. A replaced twin is
left alive (a backend may hold it in cached state): two small objects per world load.

**The look values.** `ShoreParams` (64 bytes) go to the shore program as its MaterialConstants (b1, the
kit's `PSConstants.mc`): `DrawShore` writes them into the context's material staging for its own draw (dirty
bit 0x8000) and puts retail's back after it, so the next draw item sees what it would have. The pass binds
no texture: t0 (the water's normal texture) and t1 (the frame copy) are retail's.

**The layers** (`shore_overlay.hlsl`), on a depth `d` = the frame alpha at the pixel plus a still noise (the
waterline's wander):
1. *The wet bed.* Retail lets the dry bed show through its shallows; the ground under the shallows is pulled
   toward a darker, richer shade of its own colour (refracted, as the water shows it), rising just below the
   waterline, holding, and handing over as retail's water turns opaque. Drawn as an alpha blend toward a dark
   colour, so the water's ripples on top still show.
2. *The line.* A thin line at the waterline: faint along a plain bank, white on the outside of bends. A bend
   is found by an 8-tap ring of `bendRadius` world units round the pixel: the share of taps that are land
   is about 0.5 on a straight bank and more where the bank wraps round the water. The ring is a circle on the
   water plane (the screen offsets of one world unit come from `ddx` / `ddy` of the view direction; the
   camera position cancels), capped at 96 pixels.
3. Fog fades the overlay with the water (`lightFogAlpha.z`), and the mesh's own edge alpha applies.

**Fixed to the world.** Every pattern is keyed to the mesh's still coordinate (`MeshCoord`: a blend of the
two scrolled uv sets that cancels the scroll, River `(x, 2w - y)`, Lake `(x, (0.01 y + 0.0057 w) / 0.0157)`,
scaled by 18 to about one world unit, measured against a house footprint) or to the world through the view
direction's derivatives. Keyed to the scrolling uvs, the pattern swam and glinted like a reflection at a
scale of its own; keyed to the screen, the bend test changed as the camera turned (both seen in the spot
checks). Colours come from the frame copy (the lit, fogged scene), so the overlay follows fog, seasons and
snow with no constant of its own.

**Cost.** One extra draw per water range (a few per frame) and the shore program on the water's pixels;
pixels past the wet bed return at once. Not measured (no stress A/B yet).

## 4. The look values (`ShoreParams`)

The defaults are the user's set from the debug overlay (2026-10-31). Depths in frame-alpha units (0 = the
bank, 1 = about 1.5 world units down).

| value | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `lineIn` | 0.033 | the line fades in over this depth below the (wandering) waterline |
| `lineOut0`, `lineOut1` | 0.012, 0.092 | it fades out between these (`lineOut0 < lineIn`: a narrow peak, not a plateau) |
| `linePlain` | 0.17 | its opacity along a plain bank |
| `lineBend` | 0.49 | its opacity on the outside of a bend |
| `bendRadius` | 2.24 | the bend ring, world units |
| `bendFrom`, `bendTo` | 0.414, 0.614 | the ring's land share over which the white comes in (below 0.5: straight banks keep a little) |
| `wetRise`, `wetFall`, `wetEnd` | 0.121, 0.276, 0.709 | the wet bed darkens over `wetRise`, holds to `wetFall` (its width), is gone by `wetEnd` |
| `wetStrength`, `wetShade` | 0.28, 0.25 | how far it is pulled toward the wet shade, and the shade's brightness |
| `edgeAmp`, `edgeScale` | 0.037, 0.33 | the waterline's wander: depth, and cells per world unit |
| `opacity` | 0.85 | the whole overlay |

The debug overlay (Alt+F10, `docs/reference/tweak_overlay.md`) moves every value live and prints the set to
copy back into `shore_params.h`. Release and perf builds draw with the defaults.

## 5. Verified, and not

- Windows DX11, debug: the pass draws (a diagnostic build painted the frame alpha, the masks and the mesh
  coordinate), the twins are made per world (`[BetterWater] ... shore program made`, `twin made ...` in
  `standalone_error.log`), the user's spot checks in game, clean runs with no crash log; the perf flavour
  builds. `tools/build.sh unit`: 1660 tests, 0 failures (on the rebased tree).
- Linux Vulkan (WSL2, lavapipe 1.4 / LLVM 21, debug, `tools/linux_run.sh world` with `LINUX_PROFILE=` so the row is on, seed 12345): both shore programs converted by dxbc-spirv ("2 shader module(s), 2 converted, 0 failed"), the twins made for the menu backdrop and the world, 0 validation messages, exit 0, empty crash log; the screenshot shows the same wet bed and line as DX11. The faithful route (`/profile:faithful`, row off) also exit 0.
- **Not run:** Android Vulkan (a GPU driver rather than lavapipe) and Metal; the gate
  (`tools/build.sh run`, faithful: the row is off there) and `tools/resave_gate/resave_gate.sh Gate` (the row
  is drawing only and writes nothing to the save); a stress A/B.
- **Not checked:** whether dock and bridge piles (OpaqueInWater) get the line round them from the frame
  alpha, which decides part of Fancy.

## 6. Fancy (not built)

Desktop only; a third combo item with a valued key of this row:
- contact foam for models that write no frame alpha (boats), from a readable depth (the AO depth resolve);
- a wet, darker line on the bank above the waterline, which needs the terrain shader (above the waterline
  the frame alpha is flat 0, so the water pass has nothing to measure);
- a 3D break-up of the edge (stones along the bank); flat cell "pebbles" in the overlay read as dark ovals
  and were removed.
